### Added

- Strain, Growl, Rasp, Vowel, Cup and Level controls.
- The scream engine for vocals: the voice cracks as you push past Strain, Growl adds a false-cord growl an octave down, Rasp adds breath, and Vowel and Cup shape it like a throat and a cupped mic.
- A road-worn arterial red pedal for the editor, with a footswitch and LED.
